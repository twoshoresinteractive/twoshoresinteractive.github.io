Little Bowl privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Little Bowl is developed by Two Shores Interactive, an independent developer. This policy applies to the AdMob-supported Android release of Little Bowl and this developer website. It explains how local game data, advertising information, and support communications are handled.

Information stored on your device

Little Bowl does not require an account. Its food-creation and local gallery features work on your device; loading advertisements requires an internet connection. It stores dish drafts, saved plate images, ingredient recipes used by the Feed the Cat game, and preferences such as music and cat appearance in the app's storage on your device. These creations and preferences are not uploaded to the developer.

The game does not request your name, email address, exact date of birth, contacts, precise device location, camera, or microphone. It may ask for an age range to apply appropriate advertising protections; this choice is stored locally and is not used to create a developer-held user account. It does not include chat or an in-app purchase system.

Saving and sharing images

Saving a dish creates a private gallery copy and attempts to export a copy to your device's Pictures/LittleBowl folder. Android 9 and earlier require storage permission for that export; newer Android versions use the system photo storage service. The app does not browse or upload your existing photo library.

Sharing is optional. When you choose to share a plate, Android sends that image to the app or service you select. The recipient handles the image under its own terms and privacy policy. Little Bowl does not automatically publish saved plates.

Children and families

Little Bowl is intended for children and adults. Age screening is used to apply appropriate advertising settings. Children and users whose age is unknown receive child-protective ad treatment. We do not use personalized advertising for these users or transmit their Android advertising ID. Ads served to these users come only from Google Play Families self-certified ad SDK versions and sources.

Game creations stay on the device. Ad services may still process limited technical information needed to deliver contextual ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, as described below. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no technical data is processed.

Parents and guardians can remove local data using the options below and contact us about privacy requests. Please do not send a child's name, exact age, address, photographs, or other personal information unless necessary to resolve the request.

Retention and deletion

  • Use the delete control in Little Bowl's gallery to remove a saved plate and its associated ingredient recipe from the private gallery.
  • Clear Little Bowl's storage in Android settings to remove local drafts, gallery saves, and preferences. Uninstalling also removes the app's private local data.
  • Copies exported to Photos or shared elsewhere remain separate. Delete exported copies using your Photos or Files app; contact the receiving service about shared copies.

Android backup and restore may include app data depending on your device and backup settings. The developer does not receive those backups. Removing local data does not necessarily delete an existing device backup; manage backups through your device or Google account settings.

Support messages

If you email twoshoresapps@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. We use it to answer your request, investigate reported problems, and maintain necessary support records. We retain messages only as long as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us; some information may need to be retained for legal or security reasons. Email is provided through Gmail and is also processed under Google's privacy policy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising SDKs, contact forms, or developer-set tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, including your IP address and browser information, to provide and secure hosting. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Little Bowl uses Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, to display banner ads and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks, such as after a dish is saved. Advertising helps support development. Ads are separate from your locally saved creations; we do not send dish images or ingredient recipes to Google for advertising.

Depending on the applicable age treatment, privacy choices, and SDK configuration, Google's advertising services may collect and share:

  • Your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location.
  • Device and app information, such as operating system, app version, and permitted device or app identifiers. Advertising identifiers are excluded for children and users of unknown age.
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements, such as ad impressions, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as SDK performance, app launch information, and crash or error information.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertisements, operate and secure its services, and detect invalid activity and fraud. Little Bowl requests non-personalized ads; these can use contextual information and technical storage for functions such as frequency limits, reporting, and fraud prevention. We do not add a separate gameplay analytics SDK.

Google and its advertising partners process advertising information under their own policies. See Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK disclosure. Advertising data is retained under the providers' applicable policies; clearing Little Bowl's local storage does not itself delete information held by Google.

Privacy choices and requests

Where required, the app presents a Google privacy message before requesting ads and provides a way to revisit applicable privacy choices. Consent requirements and available options depend on your location and age treatment. Non-personalized ads may still require consent for storage or other processing in some regions. If the required conditions for requesting an ad are not met, the app does not request it; its local gameplay remains available.

You can also manage available advertising and privacy settings through your Android or Google account settings. For information held by Two Shores Interactive, contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, or to raise a privacy concern. Available rights depend on applicable law. For information processed by Google, use the controls and request mechanisms described in Google's privacy policy. No payment is required to contact us about a privacy request.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when Little Bowl's information practices change. The date above identifies this policy version.

For support, privacy questions, or a parent or guardian request, email twoshoresapps@gmail.com.
